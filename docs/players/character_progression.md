# Character Progression Guide

## Overview

Character progression in our roguelike is unlike traditional RPGs. Instead of rigid class systems and artificial level caps, we offer **true freedom** with **infinite growth potential**. Your character's power is limited only by your strategic choices and willingness to explore the complex systems we've designed.

## 🎯 Core Progression Systems

### Experience & Leveling
- **No Level Cap**: Continue gaining levels indefinitely
- **XP Sources**: Combat kills, exploration, quests, crafting
- **Scaling XP Requirements**: Each level requires progressively more XP
- **Catch-up Mechanics**: New characters gain bonus XP to catch up

### Attribute System
Your character has four core attributes that define their capabilities:

#### Primary Attributes
| Attribute | Description | Combat Impact | Non-Combat Impact |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Strength** | Physical power | Increases melee damage, knockback | Carry capacity, mining efficiency |
| **Dexterity** | Agility and precision | Critical hit chance, attack speed | Stealth, lockpicking, crafting speed |
| **Vitality** | Health and resilience | Max health, damage reduction | Stamina regeneration, poison resistance |
| **Intelligence** | Mental acuity | Spell damage, mana pool | Skill cooldown reduction, item identification |

#### Derived Statistics
- **Attack Power**: Base damage from weapons + Strength/Dexterity bonuses
- **Spell Power**: Magic damage + Intelligence scaling
- **Critical Chance**: Base 5% + Dexterity contribution
- **Critical Damage**: Base 150% + Dexterity scaling
- **Health Regeneration**: Base rate + Vitality bonus
- **Movement Speed**: Base speed + Dexterity modifier

### Skill Points & Allocation
- **Points per Level**: Gain 3 skill points every level
- **No Restrictions**: Spend points on any skills, regardless of "class"
- **Respec System**: Reallocate points with a token (crafted or purchased)
- **Point Efficiency**: Early investments yield higher returns

## 🧩 The Skill Maze System

### Understanding the Maze
The skill maze is our revolutionary approach to character progression:

- **Branching Paths**: Multiple viable routes through interconnected skill nodes
- **Strategic Depth**: Early choices affect late-game potential
- **Visual Navigation**: Intuitive maze-like interface showing connections
- **Unlock Requirements**: Level thresholds and attribute prerequisites

### Navigation Strategies

#### Early Game (Levels 1-10)
```
Focus on foundational skills that enable your preferred playstyle:
├── Combat Basics (essential for all builds)
├── Resource Gathering (sustainable progression)
└── Basic Crafting (self-sufficiency)
```

#### Mid Game (Levels 11-25)
```
Specialize while maintaining flexibility:
├── Primary Combat Tree (your main damage type)
├── Supporting Skills (utility and survivability)
└── Economic Skills (better gear through crafting)
```

#### Late Game (Levels 26+)
```
Maximize synergies and unique combinations:
├── Advanced Specializations (endgame power spikes)
├── Utility Masteries (quality-of-life improvements)
└── Economic Dominance (self-sustaining builds)
```

### Key Skill Categories

#### Offensive Skills
- **Weapon Masteries**: Enhanced damage with specific weapon types
- **Elemental Effects**: Fire, frost, arcane damage modifiers
- **Critical Enhancements**: Increased crit chance and damage
- **Area Attacks**: Multi-target damage capabilities

#### Defensive Skills
- **Damage Reduction**: Flat and percentage damage mitigation
- **Regeneration**: Health and resource recovery bonuses
- **Crowd Control Immunity**: Resistance to stun, slow, fear
- **Evasion Skills**: Dodge chance and movement speed

#### Utility Skills
- **Resource Management**: Better mana/stamina efficiency
- **Movement Enhancements**: Speed, teleportation, phasing
- **Detection Skills**: Reveal hidden enemies and traps
- **Economic Bonuses**: Better prices, more loot drops

## 🏆 Mastery System

### How Mastery Works
- **Usage-Based Growth**: Skills improve through repeated use
- **Tiered Bonuses**: Increasing power at mastery milestones
- **Decay Mechanics**: Skills lose mastery if unused (prevents stagnation)
- **Cross-Skill Synergies**: Mastered skills enhance related abilities

### Mastery Tiers
| Tier | Mastery Points | Bonus Type | Example |
|------|----------------|------------|---------|
| **Apprentice** | 0-100 | Minor bonuses | +5% damage |
| **Journeyman** | 101-500 | Moderate bonuses | +10% damage, +5% crit |
| **Expert** | 501-2,000 | Major bonuses | +15% damage, +10% crit, special effects |
| **Master** | 2,001-10,000 | Superior bonuses | +20% damage, +15% crit, enhanced effects |
| **Grandmaster** | 10,001+ | Ultimate bonuses | +25% damage, +20% crit, unique abilities |

### Mastery Strategies
- **Focused Mastery**: Pour all effort into one skill for maximum power
- **Balanced Mastery**: Spread points across related skills for synergy
- **Situational Mastery**: Master skills for specific encounters or challenges
- **Economic Mastery**: Focus on gathering/crafting for self-sufficiency

## ⚖️ Build Philosophy

### Understanding Synergies
Skills work together in complex ways:

#### Damage Synergies
```
Weapon Mastery + Elemental Effect = Elemental weapon attacks
Critical Enhancement + Weapon Mastery = Devastating critical hits
Area Attack + Crowd Control = Area denial capabilities
```

#### Survival Synergies
```
Damage Reduction + Regeneration = Tanky builds
Evasion + Movement = Hit-and-run tactics
Immunity + Detection = Crowd control resistance
```

#### Utility Synergies
```
Resource Management + Offensive Skills = Sustained damage
Movement + Exploration = Efficient mapping
Economic Bonuses + Crafting = Self-sufficient builds
```

### Popular Build Archetypes

#### The Glass Cannon
```
High damage output, low survivability
├── Max Strength/Dexterity investment
├── Critical damage skills
├── Offensive masteries
└── Minimal defensive skills
```

#### The Tank
```
High survivability, moderate damage
├── Max Vitality investment
├── Defensive skills
├── Damage reduction masteries
└── Regeneration abilities
```

#### The Support Caster
```
Control and utility focus
├── High Intelligence investment
├── Crowd control skills
├── Detection abilities
└── Healing/support spells
```

#### The Hybrid
```
Balanced approach with multiple roles
├── Even attribute distribution
├── Mixed skill selection
├── Utility masteries
└── Flexible playstyle
```

## 🎮 Practical Tips

### Early Game (Levels 1-15)
1. **Focus on Basics**: Get fundamental skills first
2. **Experiment Freely**: Try different skills to find preferences
3. **Build Resources**: Don't neglect gathering and crafting
4. **Learn the Maze**: Understand connection requirements

### Mid Game (Levels 16-40)
1. **Specialize Gradually**: Commit to 2-3 main skill trees
2. **Balance Investment**: Don't neglect secondary attributes
3. **Master Key Skills**: Focus mastery on your most-used abilities
4. **Plan Ahead**: Consider endgame goals when making choices

### Late Game (Levels 41+)
1. **Maximize Synergies**: Look for powerful skill combinations
2. **Fill Gaps**: Address weaknesses in your build
3. **Economic Independence**: Master crafting for self-sufficiency
4. **Experiment Boldly**: Try unconventional skill combinations

## 🔄 Respec and Adaptation

### When to Respec
- **Build Mistakes**: Realized you chose wrong early skills
- **Playstyle Changes**: Want to try different approaches
- **Challenge Runs**: Need different skills for specific challenges
- **Meta Shifts**: Community discovers new optimal paths

### Respec Strategy
1. **Analyze Current Build**: Identify what works and what doesn't
2. **Plan New Path**: Map out your desired skill maze route
3. **Gather Resources**: Farm materials for respec tokens
4. **Execute Changes**: Reallocate points strategically
5. **Test New Build**: Verify improvements before committing

## 🌟 Advanced Concepts

### Diminishing Returns
- **Soft Caps**: Skills become less effective after certain points
- **Efficiency Curves**: Early investments yield better returns
- **Opportunity Cost**: Choosing one path means forgoing others

### Power Scaling
- **Linear Growth**: Basic skills scale predictably
- **Exponential Synergies**: Combined skills create power spikes
- **Diminishing Power**: Late-game progression slows down
- **Break Points**: Levels where builds undergo significant changes

### Economic Considerations
- **Skill Costs**: Some skills require rare materials
- **Time Investment**: Mastery requires consistent usage
- **Respec Economics**: Calculate if respec costs are worth benefits
- **Long-term Value**: Consider skills' usefulness at endgame
